# P12 — European launch and growth intelligence

P12 is the strategy/allocation layer on top of P1–P11. It does not create a second event bus, orchestrator, localization system, regulatory system or security gate. P8 invokes the daily P12 intelligence worker; P12 writes priorities and evidence-backed recommendations in `SHADOW_RECOMMEND_ONLY` mode.

The market portfolio always contains the canonical 33 markets. A market attractiveness score exists only when at least five weighted dimensions have source references. Missing data produces `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, not a zero or a fabricated rank. Market × segment opportunities remain `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` until merchant population, expected value, conversion and regulatory evidence support ranking.

Launch readiness has non-averageable gates. P11 failure yields `TECHNICAL_BLOCKED`; P10 block/review yields `REGULATORY_BLOCKED`; missing native P9 coverage yields `COMMERCIAL_BLOCKED`. Data maturity then controls research/limited status. Market activation is audited, reversible and founder-authorized for material changes.

Canonical funnel events are idempotent. First touch and full attribution history are separate. CAC requires every declared cost category plus attributed customers; missing inputs make it unknown. Revenue, verified savings, TAM/SAM/SOM and pipeline retain separate definitions. Forecasts use uncertainty scenarios and never commitments. Experiments require a pre-registered minimum sample, preserve `NO_CONCLUSIVE_RESULT` and adjust thresholds for multiple comparisons.

Allocation recommendations respect capacity, safe mode, kill switches, exploration limits, fatigue/deliverability and P10/P11. Paid spend, binding agreements, material concessions and market activation remain recommendations until explicit authority exists. Provider compensation cannot override merchant-first policy.

The Admin Growth cockpit shows the morning brief, approvals, 33-market matrix, blockers, scores and evidence boundaries. Founder OS remains the natural-language company-query layer and the existing Approval inbox remains canonical.

## Current operating mode

Cold start is valid. With P10 legal evidence and P11 production proof outstanding, the portfolio correctly reports hard blockers and does not manufacture launch-ready markets, mature CAC, forecasts or growth performance.
