# CAMBRA FOUNDER HANDBOOK — v0.79.0

Practical operating manual. For implementation detail, use the Operating Bible. For current company state, use Founder OS / Ask CAMBRA live tools.

## EN

### What do I do every morning?
Open Founder OS. Read the Chief of Staff brief, Founder Attention, critical Maintenance incidents, approvals, strategic meetings, collected/accrued money and major opportunities. If nothing material needs you, do not manufacture manual work.

### What does CAMBRA handle without me?
Routine acquisition inside active policies, standard replies/follow-ups, onboarding prompts, provider negotiation rounds inside authority, Aggregate operations, governed Recover fulfilment, maintenance sweeps, allowlisted self-healing, deterministic reconciliation, intelligence freshness and routine learning.

### What requires me?
Material/final commercial commitments, L4 approvals, contracts/signature authority, legal/tax/provider-compensation activation, high/critical security or financial exceptions, material Developer cutover/rollback and strategic decisions outside approved policy.

### What should I not operate manually?
Do not manually edit financial truth, invent payment status, manually coordinate every follow-up, bypass ECL/billing gates, bypass negotiation approvals, or merge Developer changes around CI/approval. Use the canonical domain action or Ask CAMBRA DO IT path.

### When should I intervene?
Intervene when Founder Attention says your authority is required, Maintenance cannot verify a repair, money/security/legal risk is material, a provider offer becomes binding/material, a migration needs authority, or evidence conflicts with production behavior.

### How do I stop CAMBRA?
Use Emergency Controls. SAFE MODE blocks new external communications, negotiations, new migration starts and new invoice issuance while leaving monitoring/reconciliation/evidence active. Use it for containment, investigate, verify, then explicitly restore autonomy. Acquisition/policies and API/OAuth access also have narrower controls.

### What should I ask?
“What changed?”, “What needs me?”, “Why is collected revenue here?”, “Which migrations are blocked?”, “Is CAMBRA healthy?”, “What did CAMBRA repair?”, “How does Recover work?”, “What can the negotiation agent do?”, “Show me the evidence”, “What happens if…?”

## FR

### Que dois-je faire chaque matin ?
Ouvrez Founder OS. Lisez le brief Chief of Staff, Founder Attention, les incidents Maintenance critiques, les approbations, réunions stratégiques, cash/revenus et grandes opportunités. Si rien de matériel ne vous requiert, ne créez pas de travail manuel inutile.

### Que gère CAMBRA sans moi ?
Acquisition routinière sous policy active, réponses/follow-ups standards, onboarding, rounds de négociation dans l’autorité autorisée, Aggregate, fulfilment Recover gouverné, maintenance, self-healing allowlisté, rapprochement déterministe, fraîcheur de l’intelligence et apprentissage routinier.

### Qu’est-ce qui me requiert ?
Engagements commerciaux matériels/finals, approbations L4, contrats/signature, activation juridique/fiscale de la compensation fournisseur, exceptions sécurité/finance critiques, cutover/rollback Developer matériels et décisions stratégiques hors policy.

### Que ne dois-je pas opérer manuellement ?
Ne modifiez pas la vérité financière à la main, ne contournez pas ECL/billing/approvals et ne fusionnez pas des changements Developer en contournant CI. Utilisez les actions canoniques ou Ask CAMBRA.

### Quand intervenir ?
Quand Founder Attention exige votre autorité, quand Maintenance ne peut pas vérifier une réparation, quand le risque financier/juridique/sécurité est matériel, ou quand un engagement devient contraignant.

### Comment arrêter CAMBRA ?
Utilisez Emergency Controls. SAFE MODE bloque les nouvelles communications externes, négociations, démarrages de migration et nouvelles émissions de factures tout en gardant monitoring, rapprochement et preuves actifs. Restaurez l’autonomie uniquement après investigation et vérification.

## ES

### ¿Qué hago cada mañana?
Abre Founder OS. Revisa Chief of Staff, Founder Attention, incidencias críticas de Maintenance, aprobaciones, reuniones estratégicas, cash/revenue y grandes oportunidades. Si nada material te necesita, no conviertas CAMBRA en trabajo manual.

### ¿Qué hace CAMBRA sin mí?
Adquisición rutinaria bajo policies activas, respuestas y follow-ups estándar, onboarding, rondas de negociación dentro de autoridad, Aggregate, ejecución Recover gobernada, mantenimiento, self-healing allowlisted, reconciliación determinista, frescura de inteligencia y aprendizaje rutinario.

### ¿Qué me necesita a mí?
Compromisos comerciales materiales/finales, aprobaciones L4, contratos/firma, activación legal/fiscal de revenue share de proveedor, excepciones críticas de seguridad/finanzas, cutover/rollback material de Developer y decisiones estratégicas fuera de policy.

### ¿Qué no debo operar manualmente?
No edites verdad financiera a mano, no inventes estados de pago, no coordines cada follow-up, no te saltes ECL/billing/approvals y no merges cambios Developer saltándote CI. Usa la acción canónica o Ask CAMBRA.

### ¿Cuándo intervengo?
Cuando Founder Attention requiera tu autoridad, Maintenance no pueda verificar una reparación, exista riesgo material de dinero/seguridad/legal, una oferta sea vinculante/material o haya conflicto entre evidencia y comportamiento real.

### ¿Cómo paro CAMBRA?
Usa Emergency Controls. SAFE MODE bloquea nuevas comunicaciones externas, negociaciones, nuevos inicios de migración y nueva emisión de facturas, manteniendo monitoring, reconciliación y evidencia. Úsalo para contener, investigar y verificar; después restaura autonomía explícitamente.
